## What was removed, retained, simplified — and why

**Removed outright (not deferred), per the owner's binding boundary:**
- All derivative content: v0.2 §9 (contract selection), the instrument-type-aware
  R:R split (§7.10/§8/§9.3), F&O dashboard fields, Gate 0 expired-option audit,
  option Greeks/IV, derivative identifiers in persistence. **Why:** the product
  no longer trades derivatives; keeping dormant abstractions "for later" is
  exactly the engineering-for-its-own-sake the owner is guarding against.
- The §4A.4 authoritative-halt/zero-trade-bucket proof contract and the
  common-50-symbol-watermark requirement. **Why:** per-symbol exclusion with a
  visible reason achieves the same safety (nothing unverified is used) without
  requiring a complex proof apparatus before a single bad symbol can be
  excluded — this was the correct fix to a problem I helped create (I set the
  original permissive default; ChatGPT tightened it; the per-symbol model
  developed in 033/034 is the actual right answer to both of our earlier
  positions, not a compromise between them).
- Position sizing as a built-in scanner feature (not removed from the concept,
  just not built until a measurable need exists).

**Retained unchanged, with reasons:**
- `canonical_bars`, `nse_calendar`, `structure`, `displacement` — verified by
  code inspection to have zero derivative coupling; no material defect
  demonstrated; re-litigating them now would be exactly the "endless
  engineering exercise" the owner is warning against.
- Session-reset ATR (not the rolling alternative) — changing it needs a
  controlled historical comparison first, per both prior reviews; I did not
  modify `displacement.py` in this turn.
- The shared-engine principle (live and replay call the same code) and the
  no-look-ahead/fail-closed data-integrity rules — these protect correctness,
  not complexity for its own sake, so they stay strict.

**Simplified, resolving the 027/033 disagreement (owner's F.4 ruling):**
- Full order-block lifecycle (§7.5-§7.8 of v0.2) replaced by the owner's
  6-point minimal model (4 states only: `ACTIVE/TOUCHED/INVALIDATED/EXPIRED`),
  **compared explicitly against** the displacement-origin pullback zone I
  proposed in 027 — both implemented as named, distinct, non-equivalent
  hypotheses in v0.3 §5.3/§5.4, with the simpler one winning by default unless
  backtesting shows otherwise. This resolves my disagreement with ChatGPT's
  original "keep full OB as core" position in 033 — neither of us was simply
  right; the owner's ruling is the actual resolution, and I implemented it as
  written rather than defaulting to either of our prior positions.
- R:R, coverage threshold and the zone-model choice are explicitly labeled
  **hypotheses**, not settled values, throughout v0.3 (§5.5, §6, §11) per the
  owner's correction that R≥1.5 is a research value, not production-ready.
